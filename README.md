## 🏭 Core Product Categories & Technical Solutions

We deliver robust, durable, and certified rubber solutions engineered for extreme industrial environments, construction projects, and infrastructure applications:

1. **Inflatable Rubber Airbags & Pipe Plugs**
   * *Applications*: Concrete culvert construction, marine launching, pipeline blocking, and trenchless repair.
2. **Bridge Bearings & Expansion Joints**
   * *Applications*: Highway bridges, railway viaducts, and large-scale civil engineering structures.
3. **Rubber Waterstops & Sealing Strips**
   * *Applications*: Waterproofing joints in tunnels, dams, reservoirs, and underground basements.
4. **Dock Bumpers & Marine Fenders**
   * *Applications*: Port protection, ship berthing, and heavy-duty loading dock impact absorption.
5. **Industrial & Automotive Oil Seals**
   * *Applications*: Hub oil seals, slewing bearing seals, and heavy machinery protection.
6. **Custom EPDM, NBR & Silicone Molded Components**
   * *Applications*: Custom industrial parts, water valve components, and hydraulic hose assemblies.
